How it works

After a barcode is scanned, this app opens Pin & Pop's pin search using that exact number. Stay signed into Pin & Pop in Safari, then use Pin & Pop's own Own, For Trade, ISO, or Grail controls.

If a barcode represents a mystery set instead of one individual pin, Pin & Pop may show the set or no exact result.
